The rythms





One quarter note has a length of one beat, looking down the table we can arive in the next conclusions:
1. A half note is two beats, this means that a half note are two quarter notes.
2. A whole note is four beats, this means that a whole note are four quarter notes.
3. A quaver note is half beat, this means that two quaver notes are one a quarter note.
4. A semiquaver note is a quarter beat, this means that four semiquaver notes are a quarter note.
